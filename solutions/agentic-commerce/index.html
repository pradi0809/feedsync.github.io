What is Agentic Commerce?

Agentic commerce represents the next evolution of online shopping, where AI agents act on behalf of consumers to discover, compare, and recommend products through natural conversations.

Instead of traditional search and browse, shoppers ask AI assistants like ChatGPT or Perplexity for recommendations. These AI agents need access to accurate, structured product data to provide relevant suggestions.

FeedSync ensures your products are optimized and accessible to these AI platforms, positioning your brand at the forefront of this shopping revolution.

Traditional vs Agentic Commerce

TRADITIONAL

Customer searches → Browses results → Compares options → Makes decision

AGENTIC

Customer asks AI → AI analyzes needs → AI recommends best options → Customer purchases
